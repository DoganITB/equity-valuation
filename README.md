# Equity Valuation Platform

AI-assisted equity valuation (market value, FCFF DCF, relative valuation, method selection, scenarios, recommendation) for any listed company, with live data: company search, share prices and peers from Yahoo Finance (yfinance), audited annual statements from IDX filings (via the open-source package IDX_GetFinancialStatement by celeebi, MIT), and cost-of-capital suggestions from Damodaran's public data sets.

Author: Dogan Sanlitürk (NIM 29026902), MM6026 Digital Business Valuation, SBM ITB. Worked example: PT Blue Bird Tbk (BIRD).

Data are unofficial and may be delayed or incomplete; check key figures against the audited annual report.
